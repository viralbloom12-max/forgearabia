One of the first questions Saudi business owners ask us is simple: how much does a website cost? The honest answer is that it depends on what you need. A one page site for a new local business and a full store selling hundreds of products are very different projects. This guide explains what drives the price so you can plan your budget with confidence.

## What actually affects the price

A website is not a fixed product. The cost moves with a few clear factors:

- **Number of pages and features.** A simple presence site costs less than one with booking, member areas, or online payments.
- **Custom design versus a template.** A design shaped around your brand takes more work than a ready made theme, and it shows.
- **Content and languages.** Most Saudi businesses need Arabic and English, which means proper right to left support and content in both languages.
- **Integrations.** Payments, CRM, WhatsApp, and analytics each add scope.

## Typical ranges in Saudi Arabia

These are the ranges we see most often for quality work. Treat them as guidance, not fixed quotes.

- **Simple business website:** roughly SAR 5,000 to 15,000. A clean, bilingual presence with a few pages and a contact form.
- **Custom business website:** roughly SAR 15,000 to 40,000. Tailored design, more pages, and integrations built around how you work.
- **E-commerce store:** from around SAR 20,000 upward, depending on the number of products, payment methods, and features.

Very cheap offers usually cut corners on speed, security, or bilingual quality, and you pay for it later. Our approach to [website development](/services/website-development) focuses on sites that load fast, rank well, and turn visitors into customers.

## Do not forget the ongoing costs

A website has running costs beyond the build: hosting, a domain name, and maintenance or updates. These are usually modest, but they are real, and a good partner will be clear about them from the start.

## How to spend your budget well

If your budget is limited, put it where it earns its keep. A fast, trustworthy site with clear calls to action and strong [local SEO](/services/seo-local-seo) will do more for your business than expensive features few visitors use. Start with the essentials, then grow.

If you are selling online, an [e-commerce build](/services/ecommerce-development) is worth planning carefully, since checkout and payments directly affect your sales.
